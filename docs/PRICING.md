# SellerPilot pricing model

## Premium Monthly
₹1,000/month

## Premium Yearly
₹9,600/year = ₹800/month effective

The yearly plan is 20% cheaper than paying monthly for 12 months (₹12,000/year).

The current prototype simulates subscription activation for the Shark Tank demo. No payment is collected. For production, connect Razorpay or Stripe to `/api/subscribe` and persist subscription state in a database.
